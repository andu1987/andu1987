Plant Sampling Protocol for 15N Isotope Studies

Sorghum–Mung Bean system · Fertilizer uptake (%Ndff) and biological N2 fixation (%Ndfa)

🌾 1. Sorghum

Monocot · Non-N2-fixing crop
Used as the target crop for fertilizer uptake (%Ndff) or as the non-fixing reference crop for measuring N2 fixation in legumes.

Key sampling stages

V6
TilleringEarly fertilizer uptake phase
R1
Anthesis / FloweringPeak biomass & N accumulation
R9
Physiological MaturityFinal dry matter & grain N partitioning

Plant fractions to separate

🌾 Panicle / Grain
Thresh and separate mature grain from the rachis and chaff.
🍃 Leaves
Separate upper active leaves (flag leaf) from lower / senescent leaves. Collect dropped litter from the 1 m² core sampling zone.
🪵 Stalks
Chop main stems into 2–3 cm segments for uniform drying.
🌱 Roots
Take root-crown cores to 30–60 cm depth. Rinse with deionized water, then 0.01 M CaCl2.
Why the CaCl2 rinse? It strips surface-adsorbed soil 15NH4+ and 15NO3− so root enrichment reflects absorbed N only.

🌱 2. Mung Bean

Dicot · N2-fixing legume (Rhizobium symbiosis)
Fixes atmospheric N2 via symbiosis with Rhizobium. Sampling must isolate nodules and track N remobilization to seeds.

Key sampling stages

R1
FloweringPeak active root nodulation
R3–R4
Pod FillingMaximum rate of BNF (%Ndfa)
R7–R8
Harvest MaturitySample before pod shattering to avoid seed loss

Plant fractions to separate

🫛 Pods & Seeds
Shell pods to separate pod husks from mature beans.
🍃 Stems & Leaves
Detach leaf blades and petioles from stems and branches.
🟤 Roots & Nodules
Gently excavate the primary root core (0–20 cm). Detach nodules and weigh separately. Wash roots in 0.01 M CaCl2 to remove soil mineral N.
Reference crop pairing: Always harvest nearby Sorghum plants grown under identical soil conditions as the non-fixing reference to calculate Mung Bean's %Ndfa (N derived from atmosphere).

🌾 Sorghum → reference

Non-fixing plant takes up N only from soil + fertilizer; its 15N enrichment defines the soil-N signature.

%Ndfa = (1 − atom% 15N excesslegume ⁄ atom% 15N excessreference) × 100¹⁵N isotope dilution method

🌱 Mung bean → fixer

Atmospheric N2 (≈0.3663 atom% 15N) dilutes the legume's enrichment relative to the reference.

💧
Common lab step for all fractions: oven-dry at 60–70 °C to constant weight, record dry matter, fine-grind (<0.5 mm), and analyse total N and atom% 15N by IRMS.
