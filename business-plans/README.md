# Youth poultry and vermicompost business plans (draft, 23 Oct 2026)

Developed from the BENEFIT-REALISE HwU Cluster pilot work plan "Small-scale poultry enterprises to promote dissemination of improved breeds and enhance youth employment" (project period stated as 2018-2020).

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `Poultry_Business_Plan.docx` | Diagnostic of the original work plan; full business plan (sections A-K); Original Data and Assumptions Register; financial statements; market comparison; risk register; schedule; validation tasks; references |
| `Poultry_Financial_Model.xlsx` | Formula-driven model for one youth enterprise: original budget baseline, assumptions with Base/Downside/Upside switch (Assumptions!C3), 60-month engine, annual statements, break-even, NPV/IRR |
| `Vermicompost_Business_Plan.docx` | New component (not in the original document), built around the poultry enterprises' sites and litter |
| `Vermicompost_Financial_Model.xlsx` | Same structure, with a material-flow calculation from raw feedstock to saleable product |

Evidence labels: [O] original, [V] verified (dated reference), [A] new assumption, [G] gap or contradiction. All [A] values must be replaced by local quotations and buyer evidence before investment or lending decisions.
